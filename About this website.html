About this page

How to use this website

Suburb Map

Found on the home page, our Suburb Map gives you information on each suburb in Australia's data. Just click on a suburb and the data will come through.

Search Bar

Our search bar allows you to search for certain suburbs, giving you a better look at each suburbs data. You can use this to search for other subpages as well. Currently we only display data of victoria in our search bar, but hope for future to add more state suburbs.

Housing Calculator

Found under the can you afford to live here subpage, our housing calculator allows you to check whether your annual income meets the requirements for a certain house/unit/rent price or average suburb house/unit/rent price. Currently we only display data of victoria in our calculator, but hope for future to add more state suburbs.

What is the purpose of this website?

The housing market is well known for being a volatile and unpredictable environment, and a whole generation of young people are experiencing the prospect of owning a house escape from their grasp. Our website aims to provide insight into how the average person lives in each suburb of Australia (focusing on Victoria), using data on income, rent, and sales to match the best suburb for an income level. The data collected provides per-suburb and per-local government area reports on median rents, property sales, average income, and percentage of private dwellings owned (outright or mortgage) versus rented. Together, this data can paint a picture of the housing market by suburb, showing which suburbs are priced at a premium vs. on sale. The majority of this data is from Q4 2023, the latest publicly available data for per-suburb information. The ABS Census occurred in 2021 and is not set to occur again until 2026, so we are limited in that sense.
